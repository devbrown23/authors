# Recipe Checklist

A single-file recipe app for cooking from your phone. Open `index.html` in a browser, or add it to your home screen.

- Paste in a recipe: one ingredient or step per line (numbers and bullets are stripped)
- Check off ingredients as you gather them and steps as you cook; progress is kept per recipe
- Scale servings up or down; amounts like `1 1/2 cups`, `½ tsp` and `2-3 eggs` rescale automatically
- Send unchecked ingredients to a shared shopping list, and check items off as they go in the cart
- Scan a photo of a recipe card, cookbook page or handwritten list and Claude types it in (when opened in Claude)
- Add a photo to any recipe
- Export and import a JSON backup

Everything is saved in the browser on the device you use.
